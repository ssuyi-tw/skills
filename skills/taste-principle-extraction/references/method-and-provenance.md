## Added safeguards, not quoted source doctrine

This skill adds evidence ledgers, constraints/preferences/hypotheses separation, exceptions, deduplication, counterexamples, independent holdout review, mismatch diagnosis, least-privilege collection, and human-governed promotion. These are engineering safeguards, not claims the speaker specified each detail.
